# Code of conduct

concurv is built in the open by people and, increasingly, with AI agents.
Everyone taking part in its issues, discussions and pull requests is
expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith; criticize ideas and code, not people;
- keep discussions on topic and make it easy for others to follow
  (reproductions, exact commands, evidence);
- say plainly what they do not know or have not tested;
- never harass, insult, threaten or publish anyone's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and block
participants who do not follow these rules. Report problems privately to
the maintainer through the contact on their GitHub profile.
